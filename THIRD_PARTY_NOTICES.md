# Third-party notices

Retulo source is licensed under MIT. Dependencies are installed separately; their implementations are not vendored in this source archive. These tables record the pinned distributions inspected for this candidate. Published license/notice files are included unchanged under `third_party/licenses/`; those texts retain their original terms and copyright holders.

License labels come from installed distribution metadata and do not replace the full texts. Preserve applicable notices when redistributing dependencies or binaries. The inventory includes file hashes and Python requirements.

## Provenance

This candidate is a clean source export of the local development prototype. Private Git history and operating data are excluded. The core contains no implementation copied from the research articles cited in the design notes; those articles informed the design.

The MIT text is available from the [Open Source Initiative](https://opensource.org/license/mit). Links above identify the inspected dependency versions.
